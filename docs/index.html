The honesty standard. MLB is the least predictable major sport. This system targets calibration and honest performance vs. realistic baselines, not raw accuracy — ~57–60% moneyline is a great result here, not a floor, and anything above ~62% is presumed leakage. Run line and totals near market breakeven is success, not failure. Every number below is from a real walk-forward backtest on the 2024 season (2,429 games), evaluated after 29 automated leakage tests confirmed no future information reaches any prediction.

CLV — Model vs. Real Closing-Line Market

A paid odds-API sample of 498 real 2024 games (9–14 US sportsbooks per game, de-vigged consensus). Lower Brier is better. The market wins on moneyline and run line — a modest gap, not a blowout — and the model is in a statistical dead heat with the market on totals.
